Build a polished cinematic Remotion scene for a Be STEM Ready corporate profile video.

## Goal

Create the **“Who is Be STEM Ready?”** section that plays immediately after the opening cinematic hook.

This section corresponds to approximately **00:15–00:36 in the full video**, so the scene itself is about **21 seconds long**.

The purpose is to introduce Be STEM Ready through real company photos and video footage, showing:

- real students
- hands-on STEM learning
- years of activity and growth
- technology and innovation
- the human side of the organisation

Do NOT make this look like a Canva slideshow.

The style should feel like a modern cinematic technology/education brand film.

---

## Technical setup

Use:

- Remotion
- React
- TypeScript
- 1920x1080
- 30 fps
- scene duration: 21 seconds / 630 frames
- use `staticFile()` for all media assets
- use `interpolate()`, `spring()`, `Sequence`, `AbsoluteFill`, and native Remotion animation APIs
- do not add external animation libraries unless already installed
- animations must remain deterministic

If this scene is inserted into the full video, it begins at:

`00:15`

or:

`frame 450 at 30fps`

# TRANSITIONS

Transitions must feel connected and cinematic.

Use combinations of:

- foreground wipe
- directional blur
- mask reveal
- camera pan continuation
- subtle light sweep
- match cuts
- short cross-dissolves only where emotionally appropriate

Do NOT use the same transition every time.

Do NOT use:

- cube transitions
- page flips
- dramatic zoom spins
- cartoon wipes
- excessive glitch

---

# PHOTO ANIMATION

Because several assets are still photographs, make them feel alive.

Each photograph should have a different subtle camera movement:

- slow push-in
- left-to-right pan
- right-to-left pan
- slight vertical movement
- controlled crop shift

Do not exceed approximately 8–12% total zoom.

Use easing so motion never starts or stops abruptly.

---

# MOTION GRAPHICS

Add one reusable tech overlay component.

Example:

`<TechOverlay />`

It may contain:

- fine grid at 5–8% opacity
- 1px lines
- small tracking brackets
- tiny moving dots
- optional coordinates
- occasional short scanning line

Keep opacity extremely low.

The viewer should feel the technology styling without consciously noticing the overlay.
